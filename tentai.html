In Tentai Show, the goal is to draw borders that divide the board into galaxies (see Rules tab). To draw borders, use the mouse or arrow keys:

  • A left-click on a border between cells creates a wall.
  • A right-click on an existing wall removes it.
  • You can mouse drag to continue drawing or erasing walls.
  • Once on a boundary, hold shift and use the arrow keys to extend walls.
  • On a wall, space toggles it; 1 sets; 0 or backspace clears.

The board starts light gray. A region only changes color once it is fully enclosed and contains zero or one star: red if empty or asymmetric; white / dark gray (modes 0–1) or light / dark green (mode 2) if it is a valid galaxy around a white / black star. Regions that still share the board or contain two or more stars stay gray. Assist Mode (1) adds counts; mode (2) also highlights unpaired cells inside asymmetric regions. When the puzzle is complete, black-star galaxies go very dark to reveal the picture. Use Undo to reverse moves.

Tentai Show (天体ショー, also called Astronomical Show or Spiral Galaxies) is a Nikoli original. Stars (white or black circles) mark galaxy centers; they may sit in cell centers, on edges, or on vertices. Draw borders along the grid so that:
  1. The board is divided into regions ("galaxies"), each containing exactly one star.
  2. Each galaxy has 180° rotational symmetry about its star.
  3. When finished, shade every galaxy whose star is black; the shaded regions form a picture (the "show").

Puzzles can be invoked by a numerical entry from 0-0, or through a character descriptor in the Display field. Numbered puzzles are roughly easy to hard. Puzzle 0 is reserved for a completed example.

Descriptors have the form: "WxH:<star-descriptor>" with optional ":<row-walls>:<col-walls>" hex segments for a solved board (same wall encoding as Double Choco). The star stream is left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Glyphs (at most one per cell): */O black/white center; >/E black/white east edge; V/S black/white south edge; +/@ black/white SE vertex. Blanks use - or a–z run lengths; . separates rows. Example for entry 1: 'descr'.
